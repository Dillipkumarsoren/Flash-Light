1. About Flash Light

Welcome to Flash Light

Flash Light is a simple and convenient flashlight utility application developed to provide users with quick access to the flashlight functionality available on compatible Android devices.

Our goal is to provide a clean, easy-to-use and practical flashlight experience without requiring users to create an account or complete a complicated registration process.

The application is designed with simplicity in mind. Users can access the flashlight functionality directly from the app and, where supported by their device, use additional flashlight controls.

Flash Light is intended to be useful in everyday situations where additional light may be needed, such as finding objects in dark areas, navigating low-light environments or using the phone as a basic torch.

2. Our Mission

Our mission is to create simple mobile utility applications that are easy to understand and convenient to use.

We believe that everyday utility applications should have a straightforward interface, useful functionality and a user-friendly experience.

With Flash Light, our focus is on providing a practical flashlight solution while keeping the application simple and accessible.

Our Focus:

Simplicity, convenience, usability, performance and continuous improvement.
