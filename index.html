Disclaimer

General Disclaimer

Flash Light is a utility application. Its performance depends on the hardware, firmware, Android version, and manufacturer implementation of your device.

Flashlight Limitations

Actual flashlight brightness can vary between devices. Variable brightness may not be available on devices that do not support torch-strength control.

Battery & Heating

Continuous flashlight or screen-light use can increase battery consumption and may cause the device to become warm. Stop using the feature if the device becomes excessively hot.

Safety Warning

  • Do not look directly into a bright LED flashlight.
  • Do not point the flashlight directly into another person's eyes.
  • Do not use the flashlight in a way that creates a safety hazard.
  • Do not use the application irresponsibly while driving or operating machinery.
  • Keep unsafe or excessively bright light away from children.

Screen Flashlight

Screen Flashlight uses the display as a light source. High display brightness can increase battery consumption and may contribute to device warmth.

Flashlight Safety Rules

  • Never look directly into a powerful flashlight.
  • Never shine bright light directly into another person's eyes.
  • Avoid using bright light where it could distract drivers or create danger.
  • Extended flashlight use can consume significant battery power.
  • Extended use may increase device temperature.

Brightness Control

Brightness control depends on Android device hardware and system support.

  • 25%: Lower supported torch intensity where available.
  • 50%: Medium supported torch intensity where available.
  • 100%: Maximum supported torch intensity.

These percentage values are logical control levels and do not represent a guaranteed measurement of physical light output such as lumens.

Screen Flashlight

When available, Screen Flashlight uses the phone's display as a light source.

  • Maximum display brightness varies by device.
  • Screen Flashlight may consume additional battery.
  • High brightness may cause the display or device to become warm.
  • Users should avoid prolonged use at maximum brightness.
  • The application may restore the previous screen brightness when supported by its implementation.
